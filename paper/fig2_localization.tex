\caption{Confound isolation. Node-localization AUC for the two feature variants
on the decorrelated (density-neutral) and correlated planted sets, per state,
against the shaded degree-null band. The topology-only variant (blue) beats the
null on both conditions in every state, including the decorrelated set where
density is uninformative, so it localizes by geometry rather than by
shortcutting the confound. The demographic-augmented variant (orange) is
erratic and falls into or below the null band on Maryland, where the confounded
features degrade it.}
